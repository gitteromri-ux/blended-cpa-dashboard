How CPA is calculated

Cost belongs to a cohort (InitialCampaignID + AggInitialLeadDate), not to each downstream Media×MonthBucket cell. To prevent double-counting the SQL emits cost on the first row per cohort and NULL on the rest; the dashboard sums it once per cohort.

Blended CPA (the headline number) = Σ cohort cost ÷ Σ acquisitions across every downstream media and every month-bucket in the selection.

Cell CPA in the heatmap uses the full selected-cohort cost in the numerator, divided by the acquisitions in that media×month cell. This answers “if I’ve spent $X on this cohort, what does a customer from this channel in this month cost me?”

Trade-off: cell CPA is high in the long tail because the same full cost is divided by a small slice of acquisitions. The alternative — allocating cost across cells proportionally to acquisitions — makes every cell look identical to the blended CPA and hides timing information. We prefer option 1 because comparing a cell CPA to the blended CPA tells you exactly how much that channel-month improves your unit economics.
